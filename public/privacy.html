Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Who we are

Back Alive Barbershop operates two locations in Dubai, United Arab Emirates — City Walk and Dubai Marina. You can reach us on +971 4 568 1417 or on Instagram at @backalivebarbershop.

What we collect

We collect personal information in three situations:

  • When you apply for a job with us through an advertisement on Instagram or Facebook, we collect your name, phone number, email address, a link to your portfolio or work samples, and your answers about your equipment, availability, and eligibility to work in the UAE.
  • When you contact us by phone, WhatsApp, or Instagram direct message, we keep the contact details and the content of your message.
  • When you visit this website, we collect standard analytics data such as pages viewed and general location, through Google Analytics.

Appointment bookings are handled by Fresha, a separate booking platform. Information you enter when booking is governed by Fresha's own privacy policy, not this one.

Why we collect it

We use job application details only to review your application and contact you about the role you applied for. We use enquiry details only to answer your question or arrange your appointment. We use website analytics only to understand which parts of the site are useful so we can improve them.

We do not use any of this information to build advertising profiles about you.

How we contact you

If your job application is a fit, we will contact you by WhatsApp, phone, or email using the details you gave us. If you contacted us with a question, we reply on the same channel you used.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. Your details are visible only to the Back Alive team.

Some information necessarily sits with the platforms we use to run the business: Meta (for job applications submitted through Instagram or Facebook lead forms), Fresha (for bookings), and Google Analytics (for website statistics). Each of these operates under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep job applications for up to 12 months, so we can get back in touch if a similar role opens up. We keep customer enquiries for as long as needed to serve you. After those periods we delete the information.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what information we hold about you, correct anything that is wrong, or delete it entirely. Message us at +971 4 568 1417 or DM @backalivebarbershop and we will action your request.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and understand how people move around the site. This relies on cookies stored in your browser. You can block or delete these at any time in your browser settings, and the site will still work normally.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
